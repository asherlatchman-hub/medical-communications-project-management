# Medical Communications | Account Executive Project Management Simulation

### Overview

An independent Excel-based project management simulation exploring the responsibilities of an Account Executive working within a medical communications agency.

The project models the coordination of a healthcare professional education webinar for a fictional biotechnology client, **Asteria Bio**, focusing on project delivery, stakeholder management, financial oversight, risk management and change control.

The objective was to demonstrate how an Account Executive can support cross-functional teams, identify delivery risks and prepare evidence-based recommendations for an Account Manager.

## Project Scenario

The fictional client commissioned an educational webinar on hypertension management, scheduled for **25 November 2026**, with an approved project budget of **£9,890**.

During delivery, several challenges emerged:

- A three-day delay in medical writing disrupted the scientific review schedule.
- The client requested three additional scientific slides outside the original brief.
- Design capacity constraints threatened subsequent project milestones.
- The financial forecast exceeded the approved budget.
- Scientific accuracy, client approvals and the fixed webinar date needed to be protected.

## Excel Project Management Tools

The workbook incorporates:

- **Project Tracker:** Deliverables, responsibilities, baseline and revised deadlines, task statuses and automated risk flags.
- **Gantt Timeline:** Visual scheduling and identification of dependencies and potential delays.
- **Budget Forecasting:** Approved costs, forecast expenditure and budget variance analysis.
- **Risk Register:** Delivery risks, potential impacts and mitigation actions.
- **Change Control:** Documentation of additional client requests, estimated costs and approval requirements.
- **Recovery Model:** Comparison of alternative resource allocation scenarios.
- **Delivery Dashboard:** Consolidated view of project progress and financial performance.
- **Stakeholder Communications:** Proposed internal updates and client-facing communications.

## Key Findings

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Approved budget | £9,890 |
| Forecast expenditure | £11,670 |
| Forecast budget overrun | £1,780 |
| Forecast variance | +18.0% |
| Additional freelance design option | £600 |
| Scheduled webinar | 25 November 2026 |

The analysis identified scheduling conflicts, budget pressures and dependencies between medical writing, scientific review, design and final client approval.

## Proposed Recommendations

1. Verify revised deliverable dates with the medical writing, scientific review and design teams.
2. Evaluate internal versus freelance design resources against delivery risk and additional expenditure.
3. Assess whether additional scientific content constitutes a formal scope change requiring client approval.
4. Escalate material budget and scheduling risks to the Account Manager before making external commitments.
5. Maintain accurate project documentation and protect scientific quality throughout delivery.
